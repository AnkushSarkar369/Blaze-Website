FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work with my editor?

Yes. Blaze sends your cuts straight to Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro, with Other editors and Other formats for other editors. Or skip the editor and export the cut video directly.

What's the difference between Blaze Mode and Precision Mode?

Blaze Mode is designed for quick exports. Precision Mode gives you exact control over where each cut begins and ends. You can send Precision Mode edits to your preferred editor today; direct video export from that mode is still in development.

Will it hurt my video quality?

Blaze Mode keeps your original video quality. Your footage stays on your device.

Does it need internet?

You can work on your edit without an internet connection. An internet connection is needed when exporting. Your footage stays on your device.

How many devices can I use?

One device per license key. To move it, deactivate the key from inside the app and activate it elsewhere.

Which platforms are supported?

Linux, Windows, MacOS.
