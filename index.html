Features

Small app. Sharp tools.

Blaze Mode

Instantaneous export. Blaze Mode matches the cuts to the keyframes and thus allows instantaneous exports in the quality you recorded it.

Precision Mode

Place every cut exactly where you want it, down to the frame you're looking at.

Video export coming soon. Sending to your editor works today.

Silence detection

Choose how aggressive the cuts are, how short a pause counts, and how much breathing room to keep. Expert mode gives you finer control when you want it.

See your whole recording at a glance. Drag any cut to adjust it, and jump anywhere in a click.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work with my editor?

Yes. Blaze sends your cuts straight to Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro, with OpenTimelineIO and EDL for other editors. Or skip the editor and export the cut video directly.

What's the difference between Blaze Mode and Precision Mode?

Blaze Mode is built for speed: it nudges each cut to the nearest keyframe so export is instantaneous. Precision Mode lets you place cuts exactly where you want them. Video export for Precision Mode is coming soon; sending to your editor works today.

Will it hurt my video quality?

No. Blaze Export keeps your video exactly as you recorded it. Nothing is recompressed.

Does it need internet?

Editing works offline. Exporting needs a connection so your license can be checked. Nothing from your footage is ever uploaded.

How many devices can I use?

One device per license key. To move it, deactivate the key from inside the app and activate it elsewhere.

What do I get for free?

The full editor and silence detection, plus 2 exports in total across all formats. Enough to cut a real project and decide.

Which platforms are supported?

Linux, Windows, MacOS.
