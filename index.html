Why I built it

Built on a machine that couldn't wait.

I'm Ankush. For about 95% of my years editing video, I worked on a Dell Inspiron 3542 with Intel Pentium. Renders took hours, sometimes whole nights.

Waiting was the part I hated most, so I built Blaze around one idea: spend less time trimming and more time creating. Here's the rest of the story, in the order it happened.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What can I do with the Free plan?

You can use the full editing workspace, find silence, adjust your cuts and preview the result. Exporting a finished video or sending an edit to another app requires a paid plan.

What's the difference between Blaze Mode and Precision Mode?

Blaze Mode is the quick way to turn your recording into a finished video while keeping its original quality. Precision Mode lets you decide exactly where each cut begins and ends, then send that edit—with its precise timing—to your preferred video editor. One is built for speed; the other gives you exact control.

Can I send precise edits to my video editor?

Yes. Your cut positions are carried into Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and other compatible editors, ready for you to continue editing there.

Will Blaze affect my video quality?

Blaze Mode keeps your original video quality. Your recording stays as it is.

Do I need an internet connection?

You can edit without an internet connection. You'll need to reconnect when using export features. Your recordings stay on your device.

How many devices can I use?

Blaze is licensed for one device at a time. For information about moving your licence, see the License section inside the app.

Which platforms are supported?

Linux, Windows and macOS.
